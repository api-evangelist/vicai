# Vic.ai

> Vic.ai builds AI-native software for enterprise Accounts Payable and finance teams.
> The platform automates the end-to-end AP process — from invoice capture and data
> extraction through coding, matching, approvals, and payment — so finance teams spend
> less time on manual processing and more time on work that requires human judgment.
> Vic.ai is used by mid-market and enterprise companies, integrates with major ERPs,
> and holds SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II certifications with an ISO 27001 framework.

## Product

- [Accounts Payable Automation](https://www.vic.ai/accounts-payable): The core product. Handles the full AP workflow — invoice capture from any source, AI extraction of header and line-level data, PO matching, exception handling, approval routing, and touchless processing.
- [Invoice Processing](https://www.vic.ai/accounts-payable/invoice-processing): Captures invoices from email, SFTP, API, or mobile and extracts structured data using AI trained on a large global invoice dataset. Achieves high accuracy without manual keying.
- [PO Matching](https://www.vic.ai/accounts-payable/po-matching): Matches invoices against purchase orders across multiple dimensions. Flags exceptions and routes them for resolution rather than holding up the entire queue.
- [Approvals](https://www.vic.ai/accounts-payable/approvals): Routes invoices for approval based on learned patterns and configured rules. Designed to minimize approver interruptions by only escalating what genuinely needs human review.
- [Vic Inbox](https://www.vic.ai/ap-inbox): A centralized inbox for managing inbound invoices and vendor email. Replaces shared AP email inboxes with a structured, auditable workflow layer.
- [Bill Pay & Payments](https://www.vic.ai/payments): Executes vendor payments within the Vic.ai workflow, reducing the need to switch between AP and payment systems.
- [Vendor Portal](https://www.vic.ai/vendor-portal): Gives vendors a self-service interface to submit invoices and check payment status. Reduces inbound vendor inquiry volume for AP teams.
- [Expense Management](https://www.vic.ai/expense-management-corporate-card): Manages corporate card transactions and employee expenses within the same platform as AP, giving finance teams a unified view.
- [Analytics & Insights](https://www.vic.ai/analytics-and-insights): Reporting and dashboards for AP performance — invoice volumes, cycle times, exception rates, spend visibility, and team productivity.
- [VicAgents](https://www.vic.ai/vic-agents): AI agents designed to handle discrete finance tasks autonomously — not just assist humans but complete work end-to-end within defined parameters.
